Privacy

Privacy policy.

How Rentoule Projects collects, uses and looks after your personal information. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Rentoule Projects (ABN 16 647 366 461) is a residential builder in Canberra. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you have a question about anything here, contact Jeff Rentoule on 0434 435 966 or info@rentouleprojects.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address, phone number, the address of the property, and what you tell us about your project, budget and timing.
  • If you become a client: information we need to design, price, approve and build your project, such as plans, selections, approvals documents, contracts, invoices and correspondence. Some of this is held in our client portal.
  • If you apply for a job: the details and documents you send with your application.
  • When you use this website: technical information such as pages visited, the device and browser used, approximate location and how you arrived at the site. This is collected with cookies and similar tools (see below).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and talk with you about your project.
  • To prepare proposals, quotes and contracts, and to deliver and support your build.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including building, licensing, insurance, tax and record-keeping requirements.
  • To understand how people use our website and improve it, and to measure our advertising.
  • To send you occasional updates, but only if you have agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share personal information where it is needed for the purposes above. That can include:

  • designers, engineers, certifiers, surveyors, consultants, trades and suppliers working on your project;
  • government authorities and insurers, for example for approvals and home warranty insurance;
  • our accountants, lawyers and other professional advisers;
  • service providers who host our systems, including HubSpot (enquiry forms and client records), Meta (Facebook advertising) and Microsoft Clarity (website analytics).

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Australia. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses cookies and similar technologies from HubSpot, Meta and Microsoft Clarity to remember your visit, connect form enquiries to how you found us, measure our advertising and see how pages are used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, although some features such as form tracking may not.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us using the details above. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact us first and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
