Renovations

Most Canberra homes have good bones and outdated layouts. We rework the inside without rebuilding the shell, for clients who want a home that finally fits the way they live.

That might be a whole-of-home renovation, a reworked kitchen and living area, new bathrooms and laundry, or structural changes that open the house up. Selections are made with you in person, and the cost impact is discussed as you choose.

See our recent renovations in Weetangera, Holder and Griffith, with photographs and our clients’ own words.

  • Initial consultation
  • Design and planning
  • Material selections
  • Demolition and preparation
  • Structural work
  • Trades coordination
  • Quality finishing
  • Final inspection and handover

Extensions

Extensions do more than add square metres. Done properly, they change how the whole home works day to day. New structure has to sit with old, new finishes have to respect what’s there, and you often have to live through it.

Ground-floor additions, second storeys and new living wings. Every extension gets the same planning, fixed pricing and site management as all our work, and we work closely with your architect or designer, or can introduce you to trusted local architects if you don’t have one yet.

  • Existing home assessment
  • Architect referral and coordination
  • Structural engineering coordination
  • Planning and building approvals
  • Integration with the existing home
  • Full project management
  • Interior fit-out
  • Completion and handover

Custom homes

We build custom homes, including knockdown rebuilds, for clients with a clear vision who want to enjoy the process of getting there. We take on a small number at a time, so each one gets proper attention.

Whether you’re working with an architect or starting from a blank slate, we offer clear advice from the outset and guide you through every step.

  • Site assessment and feasibility
  • Design consultation
  • Architect coordination
  • Approvals coordination
  • Full construction
  • Project management
  • Quality assurance
  • Final handover
