Campbell · Full-house renovation and extensions

A 1960s home, opened up and filled with light.

We gutted most of the house, including the kitchen, laundry and internal brick walls, and opened it right up to bring the natural light in.

Campbell · Inside the renovation

A new kitchen, a new master suite and room to breathe.

A rear extension makes room for the new kitchen, with natural stone benchtops, a more functional butler’s pantry and a servery window onto the new deck we built. At the front, a second extension adds a third bedroom and a new master suite with full-height built-in wardrobes and an ensuite.

The existing two bedrooms, main bathroom and ensuite were retained. Hydronic heating, upgraded lighting, new floorboards and carpet, and a full repaint inside and out complete the home.

“Having this incredible connection to the backyard and being able to see green and light, it just was exactly what we’d hoped at the very beginning of the project. It immediately felt like home.”
Nina & Andrew, Campbell · from our case study video

Weetangera · Whole-of-home renovation

A 1970s home, brought up to date.

A comprehensive interior overhaul of a 1970s home, made more accessible for an elderly family member. Internal walls, floors and ceilings came out, the plumbing and electrical were upgraded, and damaged wet areas were structurally repaired and fully waterproofed. New tiling, plastering, joinery, timber floors and carpet finished the home, built through the material shortages of the COVID-19 lockdowns.

“Right from the outset, it was clear that Jeff’s expertise and commitment were unparalleled as he took charge of managing the extensive renovation project. Our home, with its origins in the 1970s, required a comprehensive interior overhaul to bring it up to date and enhance accessibility for our elderly family member.”
Chris, Weetangera

The scope of work was substantial, involving the removal of internal walls, floors, and ceilings, along with extensive plumbing and electrical upgrades.

One of the standout features of Jeff’s approach was his attention to detail.
