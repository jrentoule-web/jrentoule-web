Before we talk

Worth thinking about first.

What are you really trying to achieve?

Not the renovation itself, the reason for it. More space for a growing family, a home to age in, a kitchen you can cook in properly. This is the part that shapes everything downstream.

What is your realistic budget, and your dream budget?

Both matter. The realistic one keeps the project honest. The dream one tells us where the biggest levers are.

Do you have drawings, or is it still an idea?

A sketch, a concept, full drawings or a half-finished DA. There’s no wrong answer. It tells us where we’re starting.

Who else is part of the decision?

Partner, family, a financial adviser. It helps to have every decision-maker involved from the start.
