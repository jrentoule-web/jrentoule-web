Our story

Meet Jeff Rentoule.

Hi, I’m Jeff. Canberra born and raised, and I’ve never lived anywhere else. This city is home in every sense of the word. My wife, our three kids and I live and breathe Canberra. Most weekends you’ll find us at one of the local parks, riding around the bike trails, or just enjoying everything this place has to offer.

I’ve spent 19 years in the construction industry. I started on the tools, learned the trade from the ground up, and worked on just about every kind of residential project Canberra has to offer, from cold concrete slab pours in the depths of winter to heritage extensions in the Inner North. Five years ago I took the leap and started Rentoule Projects because I wanted to do this work my way. I wanted to build the kind of relationships with clients that I’d want as a homeowner myself.

For me, this is more than a job. I genuinely care about the families and the homes I’m trusted to work on. That’s why I keep the team small, why I’m personally across every project, and why every contract is fixed-price so you always know where you stand.

Canberra is a small town in a lot of ways, and reputation is everything. Most of my work comes through referrals from past clients, and I’m proud of that. It tells me I’m doing something right.

What we stand for

Four principles. Every project.

They shape how we price, how we communicate and how we behave when something goes wrong.

01

Think Before We Build

A successful build starts well before construction. We get involved early to test ideas, understand costs, identify risks and help the design develop into something both beautiful and buildable.

02

Build It Together

Clients, architects, consultants, trades and builders all bring something valuable. We listen, share our experience and work as one team toward a home you love.

03

Calm Over Chaos

Building is complicated. Your experience of it shouldn’t feel that way. Clear next steps and steady communication, so you always know where things stand.

04

Stand Behind Our Work

We take responsibility for what we do and follow through on what we promise. Our relationship with a client doesn’t end when construction finishes.
