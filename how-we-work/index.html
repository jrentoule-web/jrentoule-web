The work before the work.

Design, consultants, selections and pricing move together, so by the time you sign, the decisions are made and the price is real.

The PSA fee is credited against your building contract if you go ahead. It only stands alone if you don’t.

Selections, in person

Cost-carrying choices are made at trade suppliers, with your architect, an interior designer or with us.

Consultants, coordinated

Good questions.

Why pay for a PSA instead of a quote off drawings?

A quote off drawings alone has to guess at everything the drawings don’t say: selections, consultant requirements, site details. The PSA replaces those guesses with real decisions and real trade pricing. If you go ahead, the fee is credited in full.

What if the final quote is over budget?

We scale back together, by adjusting scope or looking at alternatives on the biggest cost lines. That checkpoint exists so it happens before you’re committed to a contract.

How long before construction starts?

As a rough guide, two to three months for a renovation that doesn’t need approval, and four to six months for an extension or new home that needs Building Approval.

We already have an architect. Is that a problem?

Not at all. It’s common, and often better. We work with your designer through the PSA, attend design meetings where useful and give buildability and cost input as drawings develop.

Who runs selections with us?

Your architect, an interior designer you engage, or us directly if you have a clear picture of what you want. Either way, cost impact is discussed as you choose, not discovered later.
