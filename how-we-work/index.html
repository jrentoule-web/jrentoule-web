Good questions.

Why pay for a PSA instead of a quote off drawings?

A quote off drawings alone has to guess at everything the drawings don’t say: selections, consultant requirements, site details. The PSA replaces those guesses with real decisions and trade pricing, so the fixed price is based on your actual project.

What if the final quote is over budget?

We scale back together, by adjusting scope or looking at alternatives on the biggest cost lines. That checkpoint exists so it happens before you’re committed to a contract.

How long before construction starts?

It depends on the scope, how much design work is needed and whether Building Approval is required. Your ballpark proposal includes an indicative timeline for your project.

We already have an architect. Is that a problem?

Not at all. It’s common, and often better. We work with your designer through the PSA, attend design meetings where useful and give buildability and cost input as drawings develop.

Who runs selections with us?

Your architect, an interior designer you engage, or us directly if you already have a clear picture of what you want. If you don’t yet, a specialist designer will usually get you a better result.
