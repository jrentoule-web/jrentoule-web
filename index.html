Questions

Good questions.

For the full process, from first call to handover, see how we work.

How do fixed-price contracts work?

Once we’ve agreed on scope, finishes and inclusions, the contract price is locked. We do the estimating up front and stand behind it. If the scope changes during the build, variations are put in writing and approved by you before any work happens. It’s harder for me because I have to estimate accurately, but it means you know where you stand.

Do you work in my suburb?

Most of our work is in Canberra’s inner north and inner south, and increasingly the Molonglo Valley. We’re licensed in both the ACT and NSW, so we also build in Queanbeyan, Jerrabomberra, Googong and Bungendore. For the right project we’ll travel further. Where we build.

Can we live in the house during the build?

Sometimes yes, often no. Single-room renovations can be done while you live there. Whole-house, structural or major extension work is usually faster and simpler if you can move out. We’ll talk through the trade-offs at the site consultation.

How do I get a quote or consultation?

Send through the enquiry form or call me on 0434 435 966. If your project looks like a good fit, we’ll arrange a short discovery call, then a site consultation and a ballpark proposal.
