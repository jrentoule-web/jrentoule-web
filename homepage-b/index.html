FAQ

Got questions?

We understand that building or renovating a home comes with plenty of questions, and we’re here to help.

How do fixed-price contracts work?

Once we've agreed on scope, finishes and inclusions, the contract price is locked. We do all the estimating up front and stand behind it. If the scope changes during the build, variations are negotiated transparently in writing before any work happens. No surprise invoices at handover. It's harder for me because I have to estimate accurately, but it means you always know exactly where you stand.

Do you work in my suburb?

Most of our work is in Canberra's inner north and inner south - Yarralumla, Turner, Griffith, Campbell, Ainslie, Reid, Forrest, Braddon, Dickson, O'Connor, Hackett - and increasingly out in the Molonglo Valley where a lot of the new build activity is happening: Denman Prospect, Wright, Coombs, Whitlam, Molonglo and Macnamara. We're licensed in both the ACT and NSW, so we also build in Queanbeyan, Jerrabomberra, Googong and Bungendore. For the right project we'll travel further. Have the conversation either way.

How long does a full renovation usually take in Canberra?

On-site time for a major structural renovation is 6 to 10 months. But total elapsed time, from first conversation to moving back in, is usually 12 to 20 months once you factor in design, documentation, DA or SDHDC approval, and pre-construction lead times. If you're starting today, a major renovation realistically isn't finished inside 14 months. Plan accordingly.

Can we live in the house during the build?

Sometimes yes, often no. Cosmetic refreshes and single-room renovations can be done while you live there. Whole-house, structural or major extension work is much faster and cheaper if you can move out for the duration. We'll talk through the trade-offs at consultation - living through a build saves rental costs but extends timelines and tests patience. Both options have their place.
